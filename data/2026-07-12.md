### [1] [Multiband topological group-velocity control from slow light to light stopping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08055v1)
*Junhao Yang,Jiarui Wang,Jingyu Liu,Shirong Lin,Xinyuan Qi*

Main category: physics.optics

TL;DR: The paper explores the impact of next-nearest-neighbor (NNN) couplings in a Harper-Hofstadter photonic lattice, aiming to engineer group velocity for topological slow-light control. The NNN couplings open band gaps, flatten edge-state dispersions, and enable robust, broadband slow-light transport.

These couplings are shown to enhance control over group velocity, enabling functionalities like slow-light transport and multiple light-stopping states, which are critical for applications in optical delay lines and integrated photonic systems.

Method: The study introduces NNN couplings into a Harper-Hofstadter photonic lattice. Through theoretical calculations, the authors analyze the effects of NNN couplings on band gaps and edge-state dispersions. Abstract: We introduce next-nearest-neighbor (NNN) couplings into a Harper--Hofstadter photonic lattice to establish a long-range topological photonic platform for group-velocity engineering. Theoretical calculations reveal that the band gaps support band-gap Chern numbers with opposite signs. Propagation simulations demonstrate robust, topologically protected slow-light transport of counter-chiral edge states, while the presence of slow-light edge states in all three topological band gaps enables broadband topological slow light. By further tuning the NNN coupling parameter, multiple topological light-stopping states can be realized. These results establish long-range NNN coupling as an effective mechanism for topological group-velocity engineering and provide new design principles for topological slow-light devices, optical delay lines, and integrated multiband photonic systems.

Conclusion: 该方法简单、鲁棒且精度高，为OAM测量提供了一种有效的途径。

Abstract: We demonstrate an optimization of angular double-slit interferometry for accurate measurement of orbital angular momentum (OAM) of vortex beams. By scanning the dynamic double slits, the topological charge (TC) magnitude is directly determined from the oscillation frequency of the on-axis intensity. Under this optimal condition, the interference pattern exhibits the highest visibility and the measurement error is minimized. Experiments for l = 5, 10, and 15 are performed as representative examples, and the universality of this criterion is confirmed. Furthermore, by introducing an additional phase shift, the sign of the TC is unambiguously determined, as demonstrated for l = 10 and l = -15. This simple, robust method provides a high-precision pathway for OAM metrology.

### [5] [Fundamental Sensing Limits of 6G Cooperative MIMO-ISAC Networks: Joint Position-Velocity CRLB and Decoupling Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08510v1)

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于Cramér-Rao下界（CRLB）的性能界限分析方法，用于分析协作多输入多输出（MIMO）集成感知与通信（ISAC）网络。作者推导了完整的Fisher信息矩阵（FIM）和精确的CRLB，并讨论了多单静态和多双静态感知及其混合的结果。通过简化FIM和CRLB，作者提供了计算复杂度低且可优化的性能指标，并在仿真中验证了简化CRLB的有效性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的研究在分析协作ISAC网络的性能时，通常忽略了Fisher信息矩阵（FIM）中位置和速度之间的耦合效应，导致性能界限的估计不够精确。本文旨在填补这一空白，提供一个更完整的分析框架。

Method: 作者首先推导了信号级参数到状态参数（位置和速度）的CRLB变换，并展示了完整的FIM和精确的CRLB。为了降低计算复杂度，作者进一步简化了FIM和CRLB，排除了位置和速度之间的耦合项，并提供了判断简化是否有效的标准。最后，通过仿真验证了简化CRLB的普适性。

Unlike existing studies that primarily ignored coupling between position and velocity in the Fisher information matrix (FIM), we derive the full FIM and the corresponding exact CRLB. Based on the simulation results, the impact of resource and geometric parameters on position and velocity error bounds, and the validity of the simplified CRLBs is explained through the corresponding CRLB expressions.
