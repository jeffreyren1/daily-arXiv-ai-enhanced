### [1] [Toward Reliable and Accurate Predictive ISAC in Mobile mmWave Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29741v1)
*Atefeh Rezaei,Vahid Jamali,Falko Dressler*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 该论文提出了一种低复杂度、模型驱动的CSI框架，用于ISAC系统中集成了信道预测能力的波束跟踪，旨在减少CSI获取和上行反馈的开销，并通过优化波束形成问题提升信道预测和定位的准确性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在毫米波移动网络中，传统的信道状态信息获取和上行反馈开销较大，且无法满足高可靠性和高频率的需求。ISAC系统作为一种集成感知与通信的框架，为解决这些问题提供了潜在的可能性。

Method: 论文提出了一种基于预测目标感知参数的部分CSI构造方法，结合视线和非视线信道分量，并通过SCA和Schur补分解技术优化波束形成问题。此外，针对信道路径绝对相位未知的情况，设计了鲁棒的波束形成方案。

Result: 数值结果表明，相较于传统方法（如基于通信的定位与跟踪、分离的感知与通信），所提出的框架在预测精度和链路可靠性方面有显著提升，特别在严重遮挡场景下表现出更强的网络可靠性。

Conclusion: 该研究展示了ISAC在高频、易遮挡无线网络中实现可靠和自适应通信的潜力，同时验证了所提框架在实际场景中的优越性能。

Abstract: Integrated sensing and communications (ISAC) systems offer a promising framework for beam tracking, which enhances channel awareness and improves communication reliability in mobile millimeter wave (mmWave) networks. Motivated by this, we propose a low-complexity, model-driven channel state information (CSI) framework with integrated channel prediction capability in ISAC-enabled systems. To reduce the overhead of CSI acquisition and uplink feedback, the proposed framework leverages predicted target sensing parameters to construct partial CSI from both line-of-sight (LoS) and detected non-line-of-sight (NLoS) components. Exploiting this partial CSI, we formulate a beamforming optimization problem that minimizes the worst targets' Cramér-Rao bound (CRB) under per-user communication constraints. This problem is non-convex and highly non-linear; therefore, we develop an efficient suboptimal solution using successive convex approximation (SCA) and Schur-complement-based decomposition techniques, and we evaluate its complexity in comparison with other approaches. Moreover, since obtaining the absolute phase of the channel paths may require additional pilot resources, we formulate a robust beamforming design that does not assume this knowledge, and propose a solution based on sample averaging. Numerical results demonstrate that the ISAC-based robust channel prediction and localization framework significantly improves prediction accuracy and link reliability compared with conventional schemes, such as communication-based localization and tracking and separated sensing and communication. These results highlight the potential of ISAC for reliable, adaptive, and high-frequency, blockage-prone wireless networks. Furthermore, the performance of the proposed framework is evaluated under severe blockage scenarios, which further demonstrates enhanced network reliability.

### [2] [Physical-Layer Aspects of Repeater-Assisted MIMO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29846v1)
*Diana P. M. Osorio,Kohei Ueda,Anubhab Chowdhury,Hiroki Iimori,Yuto Hama,Koji Ishibashi,Erik G. Larsson*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文研究了网络控制中继器（NCRs）在无线通信和集成感知与通信（ISAC）中的应用，探讨了其物理层设计及存在的问题，提出了联合优化和增益控制框架以提高性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 网络控制中继器（NCRs）是一种低成本、频段选择性的有源散射器，能够在不依赖回程或严格相位同步的情况下重塑无线传播环境。然而，其在多输入多输出（MIMO）网络和集成感知与通信（ISAC）中的应用还面临硬件损伤、增益控制等问题，需要深入研究以优化性能。

Method: 本文研究了RA-MIMO网络的物理层设计，包括硬件损伤、增益和激活控制、双工通信、信道状态信息获取以及宽带延迟效应。此外，还探讨了ISAC中中继器如何扩展非视距（NLoS）可见性、提高目标可观测性以及增强弱回波检测。通过综合分析这些问题，提出了联合优化和增益控制框架，以解决放大的噪声、杂波、反馈和校准误差等问题。

Result: 研究发现，NCRs在RA-MIMO和ISAC中具有显著的优势，例如改善传播环境和增强感知能力，但其性能受到噪声放大和其他负面效应的限制。通过提出的优化框架，可以有效缓解这些问题。

Conclusion: 网络控制中继器在无线通信和集成感知与通信中具有巨大潜力，但需通过联合优化和增益控制来克服其局限性，以实现更高的性能增益。未来研究应进一步探讨如何在中继器群部署中实现更高效的资源分配和性能优化。

Abstract: Network-controlled repeaters (NCRs) are emerg- ing as low-cost, band-selective active scatterers that can re- shape the wireless propagation environment without backhaul or tight phase synchronization. We study physical-layer design for repeater-assisted multiple-input multiple-output (RA-MIMO) networks, such as hardware impairments, gain and activation control, duplexing, channel-state information acquisition, and wideband delay effects. We then discuss integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), where repeaters can extend non- line-of-sight (NLoS) visibility, improve target observability, and enhance weak-echo detection. These gains, however, are limited by amplified noise, clutter, feedback, and calibration errors, motivating joint optimization and gain control frameworks for swarm repeater deployments within ISAC.

### [3] [Multivariable Newton-Based Extremum Seeking with Bounded Update Rates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28728v1)
*Farzaneh Karimi,Mohsen Mojiri,Mohammadali Ghadiri-Modarres,Azadeh Mohammadpour*

Main category: eess.SY

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于牛顿的多变量极值搜索方案，通过整合已知的更新率界限，扩展了现有的有界极值搜索方法。该方法通过估计并逆置Hessian矩阵，使收敛速率与未知的Hessian无关。作者设计了适当的解调矩阵来平均意义上估计Hessian，并通过平均分析证明了算法在一般多变量静态映射下的局部指数稳定性。相对于先前仅保证实际稳定性的结果，本文提供了精确的指数衰减收敛速率，并在弱Hessian耦合假设下建立了牛顿基有界极值搜索的稳定性结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的极值搜索方法在收敛速率上依赖于未知的Hessian矩阵，导致收敛性能不佳。作者希望通过牛顿基方法消除这种依赖性，并通过整合更新率界限，提升算法的稳定性和收敛速率。

Method: 该方法结合了牛顿基极值搜索和有界极值搜索，通过设计解调矩阵估计Hessian矩阵并逆置，使得收敛速率与Hessian无关。作者使用平均分析技术证明了算法的局部指数稳定性，并在弱Hessian耦合假设下进一步确定了稳定性结果。

Result: 仿真结果表明，相对于传统的有界极值搜索方法，该牛顿基方案能够为所有参数分配相等的期望收敛速率，显著提升了收敛性能。

Conclusion: 本文提出的牛顿基有界极值搜索方法不仅消除了收敛速率对Hessian的依赖性，还通过平均分析证明了其局部指数稳定性，为极值搜索算法的设计和分析提供了新的理论支持。与现有方法相比，该方法在收敛速率和稳定性方面具有显著优势。

Abstract: We propose a multivariable Newton-based extremum seeking scheme, incorporating known bounds on update rates. The design extends the recent bounded extremum seeking scheme to the Newton-based approach, which makes the convergence rate independent of the unknown Hessian by estimating and inverting the Hessian matrix. As a vital part of the Newton-based algorithm, we design an appropriate demodulation matrix to generate an estimate of the Hessian in an average sense. The local exponential stability of the bounded extremum seeking algorithm is proven for general multivariable static maps using averaging analysis. In comparison with previous practical stability results, it guarantees convergence to a neighborhood of the extremum point with an exact convergence rate exhibiting exponential decay. Subsequently, the main stability result is established for the Newton-based bounded extremum seeking approach under a weak Hessian coupling assumption. Simulation results demonstrate the advantage of the proposed approach over bounded extremum seeking by assigning equal, desired convergence rates to all parameters using the Newton approach.
