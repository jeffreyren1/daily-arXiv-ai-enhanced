### [1] [Cross-Region Cooperation of UAV Swarms Towards Air-Ground ISAC via Hierarchical Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31054v1)
*Linghui Miao,Nhan Thanh Nguyen,Shijian Gao*

Main category: eess.SY

TL;DR: 该论文针对无人机群在空-地网络中的综合感知与通信问题，提出了跨区域合作框架，通过资源再分配和感知协作提升服务质量和感知精度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单一无人机的通信资源和感知视角有限，而在多区域场景中，区域无人机分配可能无法匹配需求，局部观测也缺乏多样性，这影响了无人机的通信服务和感知精度。

Method: 作者提出了跨区域无人机再分配策略以减少服务差异，并结合不同区域的观测数据提升感知多样性；通过基于边缘克拉美-罗界（CRB）的自适应握手协议平衡同步增益和能耗。进一步，通过分层多智能体强化学习（H-MARL）方法协调慢速跨区域无人机再分配和快速区域内无人机控制。

Result: 仿真结果表明，资源再分配可在资源需求严重不匹配时提升服务质量（QoS）超过20个百分点；自适应握手协议下的协作感知仅导致平均CRB增加约5.0%，接近完美同步的性能。

Conclusion: 论文提出的跨区域合作框架显著改善了无人机群在通信和感知任务中的性能，为多区域无人机网络的实际应用提供了有效的解决方案。

Abstract: The limited communication resources and sensing perspectives of a single uncrewed aerial vehicle (UAV) motivate UAV swarms for integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) in air-ground networks. However, in multi-region scenarios, regional UAV allocations may not match vehicle demand, while local observations may lack sufficient diversity for accurate sensing. To address these issues, this paper proposes a cross-region cooperation framework for UAV swarms. For communication, cross-region UAV reallocation reduces service disparities by matching resources to vehicle demand. For sensing, combining UAV observations from different regions increases observation diversity, while adaptive handshaking guided by marginal Cramér--Rao bound (CRB) utilities balances phase synchronization gains against handshaking energy consumption. Joint optimization of UAV positions and transmit power within each region then further improves communication service and sensing performance. These coupled decisions are coordinated by a hierarchical multi-agent reinforcement learning (H-MARL) approach through slow-timescale inter-region UAV reallocation and fast-timescale intra-region UAV control. Under the considered simulation settings, reallocation can improve quality of service (QoS) by over 20 percentage points compared with retaining the initial allocation under severe resource-demand mismatches. Cooperative sensing with adaptive handshaking incurs only about a 5.0% increase in mean CRB relative to perfect synchronization.

### [2] [Stochastic Geometry Analysis and Optimization for Multi-LEO Satellite-Assisted Communications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31373v1)
*Xu Hu,Bin Lin,Ping Wang,Xiao Lu,Weihua Zhuang,Shuang Qi*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 为了解决单颗低地球轨道（LEO）卫星通信不可靠的问题，本文提出了一种多LEO卫星通信网络（mLEO-CN）方案，采用串行通信机制和随机几何工具分析性能，并优化传输成功率与端到端延迟的比例。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单颗LEO卫星的通信可靠性受制于复杂的卫星-地面传播和非可忽略的卫星故障概率，因此需要一种多卫星协作的方案来提升通信可靠性。

Method: 作者通过将卫星位置建模为球面上的二项点过程，并采用串行通信机制（即在前序卫星无法满足信号质量要求时才激活后续卫星），利用随机几何工具推导了包括传输成功率、平均传输速率和端到端延迟在内的性能指标。此外，作者还利用SR衰落模型的渐进表达式和Gamma分布优化分析，提出了集群近似方法，并通过一维搜索方法优化了传输成功率与延迟的比例。

Result: 数值结果验证了分析的正确性，揭示了关键参数的影响，并找到了不同星座高度下的最优服务卫星数量。

Conclusion: 多LEO卫星串行通信机制显著提升了通信可靠性，且通过优化可以找到最优的卫星服务数量，适用于不同高度的星座配置。

Abstract: Low Earth orbit (LEO) satellites are vital in providing seamless connectivities to ground users (GUs). However, complex satellite-to-ground propagation and non-negligible satellite failure probabilities make single-satellite transmission unreliable. To address this issue, we consider a multi-LEO-satellite communication network (mLEO-CN), where a typical GU is served by multiple LEO satellites. The satellite locations are modeled as a binomial point process on a sphere and ordered according to their distances to the GU. To improve communication reliability, a serial communication scheme is adopted, in which the subsequent satellite is activated only when all preceding satellites fail to satisfy the target signal-to-noise ratio threshold. By employing the tool of stochastic geometry, we derive key performance metrics, including overall transmission success probability, average transmission rate, and end-to-end delay under the serial communication scheme by incorporating both shadowed Rician (SR) fading and satellite failure probabilities. To improve analytical tractability, we derive asymptotic expressions for the end-to-end transmission success probability in two special cases by leveraging the asymptotic expression of the SR fading model and a Gamma-distribution, and further propose a cluster-based approximation approach for the overall transmission success probability. Building on these analyses, we formulate an optimization problem to maximize the ratio of the overall transmission success probability to the end-to-end delay, and obtain its solution using a one-dimensional search approach. Numerical results validate the analysis, reveal the impact of key parameters, and identify the optimal number of serving LEO satellites under different constellation altitudes.

### [3] [Array Rotation in Near-Field ISAC: Communication, Detection, and Estimation Under Low-Complexity Transmit Architectures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31136v1)
*Marziyeh Soltani,Mark F. Flanagan*

Main category: cs.IT

TL;DR: 该论文研究了近场集成感知与通信（ISAC）中可旋转基站阵列在精确球面波传播模型下的性能。作者通过CRB分解将目标端几何与发射协方差相关项分离，并比较了两种低复杂度架构的性能。结果显示，单流叠加架构能更好地协调通信和检测任务，而双波形分割架构则表现出明显的通信与检测权衡。数值模拟验证了旋转阵列的优势，特别是在距离估计方面。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 近场集成感知与通信（ISAC）是未来无线通信系统中的关键技术之一，但其性能受限于阵列几何和传播模型。传统的固定阵列设计无法灵活应对不同任务需求，而可旋转阵列为解决这一问题提供了新的可能性。作者希望通过精确建模和理论分析，揭示不同架构在旋转条件下的性能差异，并为实际系统设计提供指导。

Method: 作者采用了精确的球面波传播模型，推导了Cramér--Rao界（CRB）的分解形式，将目标端几何与发射协方差相关项分离。随后，研究集中于两种低复杂度架构：单流叠加架构和双波形分割架构。通过理论分析，作者分别给出了通信、检测和估计任务的最优旋转角度，并提出了二阶近似以简化计算。此外，作者还通过相关高斯扰动模型研究了目标状态不确定性对波束成形设计的影响，并推导了最优稳健旋转角度的近似解。

Result: 分析表明，两种架构在旋转条件下表现不同：单流设计能够协调通信和检测任务，而双波形设计则表现出直接的通信与检测权衡，且在优先感知时通信性能损失更大。数值结果显示，旋转阵列的性能显著优于固定阵列（尤其是在距离估计方面），且近似解与精确解的结果高度吻合。此外，检测任务对目标状态误差的敏感性低于估计任务。

Conclusion: 论文通过理论和数值分析证明，可旋转阵列在近场ISAC系统中具有显著优势，尤其是在距离估计方面。单流叠加架构适合需要协调通信和检测的场景，而双波形分割架构则适合需要明确权衡的场景。作者的研究为近场ISAC系统的实际设计提供了重要参考。

Abstract: This paper studies near-field integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) with a rotatable base-station array under an exact spherical-wave propagation model. For a general transmit covariance, we derive a Cramér--Rao bound (CRB) decomposition that separates target-side geometry from transmit-covariance-dependent terms. We then specialize the framework to two representative low-complexity architectures: a single-stream superposition architecture, and a dual-waveform split architecture design. We characterize the communication-, detection-, and estimation-optimal rotations for each architecture using the exact finite-array model. In addition, we show how a second-order approximation yields compact approximate expressions for the optimal rotation angles. We also study uncertainty in the target state used for beamforming design through a correlated Gaussian perturbation model, and derive approximations of the optimal robust rotation angles for target sensing and detection. The analysis shows that the two architectures behave differently under rotation: the single-stream design can align communication and detection tasks, whereas the dual-waveform design exhibits a direct communication--detection tradeoff and can incur a larger communication loss when sensing is prioritized. Numerical results show clear gains over a fixed array orientation, especially for range estimation, and close agreement between the approximate and exact rotation choices. Detection is less affected by target-state errors than estimation in the considered cases.

</details>
