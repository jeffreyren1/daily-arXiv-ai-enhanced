### [1] [Barrier Function Conformal Safety Clearance Certification with CVaR for Driving Trajectory Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26533v1)
*Pei Yu Chang,Qadeer Ahmed*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 该论文提出了一个框架，用于评估自动驾驶运动规划器生成的轨迹的实际安全裕度，并通过可微分分离轴屏障边际和后续的校准方法，确保规划时的安全统计证书与实际安全裕度的一致性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自动驾驶的运动规划器在生成候选轨迹时，通常仅评估轨迹的交互性，而缺乏对其实际安全裕度的严格认证。这一漏洞可能导致规划器在动态环境中选择不安全的轨迹。因此，作者旨在解决这一问题，提出一种能够验证轨迹安全性的方法。

Method: 作者通过可微分分离轴屏障边际确保证书的安全性，该方法对精确的定向边界盒（OBB）安全裕度提供了确定性下界。在规划时，根据名义预测和采样的尾部条件风险值（CVaR）评估边际；在轨迹选择后，使用共形校准方法校正预测和采样误差。共形校准独立于预测器的正确性，提供统计有效的安全证书。

Result: 在300次nuPlan测试中，采用该方法在10%的目标错误覆盖率下，采样的尾部CVaR将共形校正从1.43米降至0.03米，并将非负安全裕度证书的比率从68.7%提升至87.3%。在所有评估的统计数据中，精确安全裕度的覆盖率达到93.3%至96.7%，高于90%的目标。

Conclusion: 该论文提出的方法有效弥补了运动规划器在安全认证方面的不足，通过数学工具和统计方法显著提升了轨迹安全裕度的可靠性和覆盖率。这一成果为自动驾驶系统的安全保障提供了新的理论基础和技术支持。

Abstract: Autonomous driving motion planners generate and select candidate trajectories while accounting for interactions with surrounding agents. However, these evaluations do not certify the actual safety clearance of the selected trajectory. The framework evaluates the trajectory selected by ant planners and calibrates the gap between its plan time margin and realized safety clearance. A differentiable separating axis barrier margin deterministically lower bounds exact signed oriented-bounding-box (OBB) safety clearance, connecting the statistical certificate to safety margin. At plan time, the margin is evaluated using either a nominal prediction and sampled lower tail Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR), while post-selection conformal calibration over exchangeable drive sessions absorbs prediction and sampling errors. Conformal calibration provides statistical validity independently of predictor correctness. The method is evaluated on a frozen 300 session nuPlan study using native Predictive Driver Model (PDM) Closed loop proposals. At 10% target miscoverage, sampled lower CVaR reduces the conformal correction from 1.43m to 0.03m and increases the rate of nonnegative safety clearance certificates from 68.7% to 87.3%. Across all evaluated statistics, exact-clearance coverage remains above the 90% target at 93.3--96.7%.

### [2] [Radio Imaging and Resource Allocation in Frugal Multistatic D-MIMO ISAC Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27041v1)
*Sauradeep Dey,Musa Furkan Keskin,Dario Tagliaferri,Gonzalo Seco-Granados,Henk Wymeersch*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 该论文提出了一种基于分布式MIMO（D-MIMO）的下行链路集成传感与通信（ISAC）框架，通过正交子载波分配消除传感与通信信号之间的互干扰，优化资源分配以实现通信和成像的双重目标。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在分布式MIMO的ISAC系统中，通信和传感信号的相互干扰是一个主要问题。传统的信号叠加方法无法有效解决这种干扰。因此，作者旨在设计一种正交子载波分配方案，以消除互干扰，同时实现高效的通信性能和高质量的无线电成像。

Method: 论文提出了一种两时间尺度的资源分配框架。长时间尺度优化（LTO）确定接入点模式、子载波分配（包括通信与传感的资源分配）以及传感子载波在发送接入点之间的分配；短时间尺度优化（STO）则动态调整通信与传感的功率分配因子以满足当前场景的通信需求。采用了通信优先的策略，确保通信性能后再优化成像质量。

Result: 数值结果表明，正交子载波分配方法相比传统的信号叠加方法，在通信和成像之间实现了更好的权衡。此外，论文还评估了相干和非相干成像接收器在不同同步条件下的成像和定位性能。

Conclusion: 正交子载波分配在ISAC系统中能有效消除通信与传感信号间的互干扰，同时优化资源分配策略提升系统性能。相干和非相干成像接收器在不同同步条件下各有优势，需要根据具体场景选择合适方案。

Abstract: Emerging integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) systems based on distributed MIMO (D-MIMO) enable radio imaging by exploiting spatial diversity across multiple access points (APs). However, joint sensing and communication introduce mutual interference between communication and sensing signals. In this paper, we propose a downlink D-MIMO ISAC framework that allocates orthogonal subcarriers to sensing and communication to eliminate inter-function interference. We consider a phase-coherent architecture in which single-antenna APs serve communication user equipment (UEs) while constructing a reflectivity image of the environment. We develop a two-timescale resource allocation framework that minimizes reconstructed-image entropy subject to a communication spectral-efficiency (SE) constraint. The proposed design follows a communication-centric policy, where imaging uses the resources left after satisfying the communication requirement. The long-timescale optimization (LTO) determines AP modes and subcarrier assignment, including the partitioning between communication and sensing and the allocation of sensing subcarriers among transmit APs, using synthetic scenarios with random UE and target distributions. The short-timescale optimization (STO) adapts the communication-sensing power-splitting factor to preserve the SE constraint in the current scenario. Numerical results show that the proposed orthogonal subcarrier allocation achieves a superior sensing-communication trade-off compared with conventional superposition, where sensing and communication share the same subcarriers. Finally, we evaluate coherent and non-coherent imaging receivers and identify the synchronization regimes in which each approach provides better imaging and localization performance.

### [3] [The Effect of Geometry on Thermodynamic Response](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26898v1)
*Bojana Bokic,Sébastien R. Mouchet,Biljana Stankov,Sanja Ostojic,Nicolas Roy,Darko Vasiljevic,Yin Chang,Marija Radmilovic-Radjenovic,Branislav Radjenovic,Thierry Verbiest,Mohamed Hatifi,Branko Kolaric*

Main category: cond-mat.mes-hall

TL;DR: 作者旨在解决纳米和微米尺度上复杂几何结构对热辐射和热管理的影响问题，并探索通过几何设计而非化学改变来调控热响应的方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在纳米和微米尺度上，图案结构对热和光学响应的影响至关重要，不仅对生物物种的生存有重要意义，还对热管理、光谱学、光电子学和能源转换技术等应用领域具有广泛影响。因此，阐明纳米尺度热辐射的物理学机制成为迫切需求。

Method: 通过实验和理论分析，研究了复杂纳米几何结构对热管理的具体影响，尤其是对热容量测量的异常热响应。作者强调了通过几何设计调控热响应的可能性。

Result: 研究发现，复杂纳米几何结构会导致热容量测量中出现异常的热响应，并且这种响应可以通过几何设计进行调控，而无需改变系统的化学组成。

Conclusion: 通过几何设计可以调控热响应，这为解决纳米尺度热管理问题提供了新的思路，同时为热辐射和能源转换技术的发展奠定了基础。

Abstract: At the nano- and microscale, various patterns influence and shape thermal and optical response, making them essential for the survival of various biological species. In addition, controlling thermal radiation is vital for a broad range of applications, such as thermal management, spectroscopy, optoelectronics, and energy conversion technologies. For this reason, there is strong pressure to elucidate the physics of thermal radiation at the nanoscale. In this article, we provide evidence that complex nanoscale geometries affect thermal management, leading to an unusual thermal response in heat-capacity measurements as a function of temperature. Beyond identifying the structural constraints associated with this unusual thermodynamic response, the current study introduces the possibility of shaping the apparent heat-capacity response through geometry without necessarily altering the system's chemistry.

</details>
